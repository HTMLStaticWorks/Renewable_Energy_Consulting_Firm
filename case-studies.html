Client Success Stories

Read the detailed journeys of organizations that successfully completed their green energy transition with Aethera.

AH

Apex Heavy Industries

Manufacturing • Ohio, USA
Challenge: High Tariff Exposure

Apex faced severe exposure to peak-tariff pricing and an internal mandate to reduce scope 1 carbon emissions by 30% by 2025.

Solution: Solar & BESS Microgrid

Aethera designed a 12MW rooftop solar array paired with a 5MWh Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) and smart dispatch controller.

"Aethera's design eliminated our peak-tariff exposure and saved us $1.8M in Year 1."

— Marcus Vance, VP of Operations
Solar Transition 42% ROI
NP

Northern Power Co.

Utility • Denmark
Challenge: Wind Wake-Loss Inefficiency

A multi-megawatt onshore wind farm experienced high wake losses, causing severe load imbalance and a 15% drop in generator efficiency.

Solution: CFD Aerodynamic Tuning

Aethera performed high-fidelity fluid simulations to optimize turbine configurations and wake-shaping algorithms.

"Their AI wake models unlocked approval times and increased energy production by 18%."

— Sarah Jenkins, Lead Wind Engineer
Wind Strategy 18% Gen Boost
RV

Riverdale Council

Public Sector • Pacific Northwest
Challenge: Grid Outage Vulnerability

Remote communities experienced frequent storm outages and high transmission costs from legacy centralized utilities.

Solution: Low-Impact Hydro Station

Aethera designed a low-impact micro-hydro turbine system delivering steady 24/7 baseload power with zero reservoir flooding.

"Aethera brought energy independence to our town with an environmentally harmless design."

— Siddharth Kumar, Council Member
