Solar Energy Consulting

Solar feasibility studies, ROI analysis, and implementation planning.

Overview

Transitioning to solar energy represents one of the highest yielding capital investments a company can deploy. However, structural and grid connectivity errors frequently lead to over-budget installations and under-performing arrays. Aethera acts as your independent engineering representative to model and secure your transition from day one.

Core Benefits

Cost Reduction

Clients experience an average 40% reduction in annual utility bills post commissioning.

Tax Abatements

Lock in up to 30% capital tax reductions using clean investment structures.

Transition Workflow

We coordinate the solar transition across a 4-phase technical sequence to guarantee performance:

  • 1
    LiDAR Roof Modeling We run laser scans on roof structures to measure shading, tilt optimization, and load-bearing constraints.
  • 2
    Developer RFP Sourcing We draft the equipment parameters and run bidding procedures across 10+ certified local solar contractors to minimize capital premiums.
  • 3
    Grid Interconnection Management We handle paperwork and telemetry wiring alignments with the local power utility to ensure seamless net metering operations.
